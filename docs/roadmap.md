# Introduction

This roadmap describes the planned evolution of the Command Economy of AI (CE-AI) framework.

CE-AI is an evolving open research and technical initiative exploring how commands can become a coordination layer between human intent, AI agents, workflows, and digital execution systems.

The roadmap represents a direction of development and research rather than a fixed implementation timeline.

# Long-Term Vision

The long-term vision of CE-AI is to explore a future where humans communicate objectives through structured commands, and AI systems coordinate the necessary reasoning, tools, workflows, and execution paths to achieve measurable outcomes.
