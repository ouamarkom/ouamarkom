## 1. Purpose

This document records the documented evolution of Ouamarkom™ from its early conceptual foundations through the development of the Prompt Economy, Intent Economy, Command Economy, Command Economy of AI (CE-AI™), research infrastructure, architectural frameworks, Smart Hand™, CEP, intellectual-property documentation, and AI execution infrastructure.

It is maintained as a historical reference for:

- project evolution;
- research development;
- conceptual development;
- architectural development;
- publication history;
- intellectual-property documentation;
- technical positioning;
- public reference materials;
- provenance analysis; and
- future priority and evidence mapping.

This document is a historical project record.

It is **not**, by itself:

- a patent application;
- a patent claim;
- a legal opinion;
- a trademark registration;
- a determination of legal priority; or
- a statement of enforceable intellectual-property rights.

Dates associated with website pages in this document are based on the project's published sitemap `lastmod` values supplied for this timeline, unless a separate repository, publication, or archival record establishes a more specific date.

# 2. Executive Evolution

The documented evolution of Ouamarkom can be understood as a progression through the following conceptual and technical layers:

```text
Prompt Engineering
        ↓
Prompt Economy
        ↓
Intent Economy
        ↓
Command Economy
        ↓
Command-Based AI Coordination
        ↓
Execution-Oriented AI
        ↓
Smart Hand™
        ↓
CE-AI Research Framework
        ↓
Execution Architecture
        ↓
CEP
        ↓
Research + IP Ecosystem
        ↓
AI Execution Infrastructure™
```

The central strategic evolution was from treating language and prompts primarily as interaction mechanisms toward treating human intent, commands, AI coordination, execution, and outcomes as components of an emerging technical and economic infrastructure.
