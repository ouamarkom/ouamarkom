Ouamarkom™ Introduces Smart Hand™ Architecture to Develop a New Category of AI Execution Infrastructure

A model-agnostic execution layer connecting AI intelligence with enterprise systems and transforming human intent into governed, verifiable execution.

Riyadh, Saudi Arabia - August 2026

Ouamarkom™ today announced the development of its Smart Hand™ architecture, a model-agnostic execution layer designed to create an operational bridge between artificial intelligence models and the digital and enterprise systems where business processes and economic activities are executed.

The initiative forms part of Ouamarkom’s broader vision for the Command Economy of AI™ (CE-AI), which views human commands and natural-language intent as inputs that can be transformed, through a governed execution layer, into measurable operations, actions, and outcomes.

From Intelligence to Execution

As artificial intelligence models continue to advance in their ability to understand complex instructions, plan tasks, and use tools, Ouamarkom believes the next challenge is not solely increasing model capabilities, but building the operational infrastructure that enables organizations to use those capabilities in a secure, governed, auditable, and measurable manner.

Smart Hand™ is designed to address this gap through an execution layer that includes, according to the platform’s target architecture:

  • Understanding intent and objectives.
  • Translating objectives into executable commands.
  • Building interconnected execution plans.
  • Orchestrating AI agents.
  • Routing tasks to appropriate models.
  • Applying authorization and execution policies.
  • Connecting with enterprise systems and APIs.
  • Verifying the execution of actions.
  • Measuring outcomes.
  • Maintaining operational memory of decisions and execution.
  • Supporting closed-loop execution and continuous improvement.

A Governed Execution Layer

Smart Hand™ is designed as a governed execution layer for examining commands, applying execution policies, and verifying actions and outcomes according to rules and permissions defined by the organization.

This approach aims to place governance and execution control within the operational architecture itself, rather than treating them as a separate layer from the process of transforming user intent into action across digital systems.

Model-Agnostic Architecture

Smart Hand™ is designed around the principle of Model-Agnostic Execution, meaning that its execution thesis is not dependent on a single artificial intelligence model.

Subject to the platform’s architecture and approved integrations, the layer can work with commercial or open-source models, enabling organizations to select appropriate models based on factors such as:

  • Task requirements.
  • Performance.
  • Cost.
  • Privacy.
  • Enterprise requirements.
  • Governance constraints.

Ouamarkom is therefore not positioning itself around a single model winning the AI market, but around a broader premise:

«As AI models become more capable, the need grows for an independent layer that governs how that intelligence is translated into enterprise execution.»

From Command to Outcome

Smart Hand™ seeks to move beyond the traditional workflow model toward a comprehensive, process-oriented execution architecture:

Traditional Paradigm
Prompt → Response
Smart Hand™ Paradigm
Intent ➔ Command ➔ Plan ➔ Authorization ➔ Execution ➔ Verification ➔ Outcome ➔ Feedback

This places measurable outcomes at the center of the execution experience rather than limiting the interaction to generating a response or invoking a tool.

«We are not building another AI model; we are building the execution layer that enables organizations to use the right model to transform intent into commands, commands into actions, and actions into verifiable outcomes.»

Intellectual Property Strategy

Ouamarkom is developing an intellectual property strategy focused on technical mechanisms associated with AI execution infrastructure, including areas such as:

✓ Model-agnostic execution
✓ Translating intent into executable commands
✓ Command and agent orchestration
✓ Policy and authorization enforcement
✓ Execution verification
✓ Execution evidence management
✓ Outcome measurement
✓ Closed-loop execution

The company is evaluating, in collaboration with specialized advisors, appropriate pathways for protecting eligible innovations through relevant intellectual property rights, including domestic and international patent filings where applicable requirements for novelty, inventive step, industrial applicability, and other legal criteria are satisfied.

Ouamarkom aims to build a global technology company from Saudi Arabia, with a focus on developing knowledge, engineering, intellectual property, and operational capabilities within the Kingdom while building a platform capable of expanding into global markets.

This supports the company’s ambition to build secure and scalable enterprise execution infrastructure from Saudi Arabia for global markets.
