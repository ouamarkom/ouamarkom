About Ouamarkom
Research and Engineering for Governed AI Economic Agency

We research and test what AI economic agency requires inside institutions: clear delegation and defined boundaries, evidence that establishes what happened, and outcomes that can be verified.

Who We Are

Where AI Capability Meets Institutional Reality

AI is moving from generating information toward taking actions with real consequences inside institutions and real economic systems. With that shift, the question changes: from "what can the system do" to "what is it permitted to do, under what authority, and how can any party know what happened."

By governed economic agency, we mean AI actions with economic consequence carried out under delegation, within boundaries, with evidence, and with outcomes that can be verified. We start with the question, then look for the requirements that experiments and evidence reveal, and only then decide what is worth building.

"How does the move from AI capability to institutional action become governed, executable, measurable, and economically viable?"

The entire company is organized around this central question.

What We Study

AI Economic Action Inside Institutions

Our scope is narrower than "what can AI do" and broader than "how do we build a better agent." We study what changes once AI actions carry economic consequences, and what has to be true before institutions can trust them.

Authority and Delegation

How authority is delegated to an AI system, bounded by policy, and kept accountable to people.

Execution and Evidence

How an action is carried out across systems, and how what actually happened is recorded in a way that can be examined.

Outcomes and Verification

How we separate successful execution from a verified economic outcome, and how each is measured.

Action Across Systems

What changes when an economic action extends across more than one system, agent, or institution.

CE-AI™ is an evolutionary research framework we use to study the economic and institutional implications of AI's growing ability to act. It frames the question, not the answer.

Every answer opens a new question. That is why we do not begin by designing infrastructure; we begin by discovering whether it is required at all.

How We Work

Research and Engineering in One Cycle

We turn hypotheses into experiments that can be executed and measured. For us, engineering is a way to test what we think may be required, not a way to prove that the architecture we started with was correct.

We Build to Test

Systems are built to expose hypotheses to real or controlled operating conditions, not to confirm a design chosen in advance.

We Measure Execution and Outcome Separately

Completing an action does not mean its economic effect was achieved. A system can be capable without being authorized, and execution can succeed without the outcome being realized. We evaluate each independently.

We Document While We Work

Hypotheses, experiments, evidence, and decisions are recorded as they happen, including what did not work.

Experimental System | Smart Hand™

A Testing Environment, Not a Final Result

Smart Hand™ is our primary experimental system, one of the environments where we test what governed AI economic actions actually require. It is not the infrastructure itself, and it is not proof that the infrastructure exists. Its value is in showing what holds up under testing and what needs to change or be removed.

How We Learn From Evidence

Evidence Guides Change, It Does Not Force It

Evidence determines what stays. A single result does not automatically change the research framework or the architecture, so we separate what was observed, what it might mean, and what should be done about it.

01

Evidence

What actually happened?

02

Interpretation

What might that mean?

03

Inference

How does it compare against alternatives and the baseline?

04

Decision

What do the results justify?

05

Evolution

What should change?

For us, architecture is a record of what we learned. A component is added when evidence reveals a requirement, and it is modified, merged, deferred, or removed when evidence shows it adds no independent value. Every material change should trace back to a documented evidence trigger; we do not add a layer or a protocol before evidence and a recurring operational need justify it.

We Change Experiments and Systems Faster Than the Thesis

Experiments and systems may change with every cycle. The thesis changes only when evidence justifies it.

Abandoning a Hypothesis Is a Research Result

If existing systems and protocols turn out to meet the requirement without the component we proposed, the decision not to build it is knowledge gained: an uncertainty removed.

"Evidence before architecture."
"We do not assume the infrastructure. We discover the requirement."
