Why Now?

The global technology landscape is experiencing three primary technical and economic shifts:

  • AI Model Maturity: Advanced reasoning and multi-step inference capability in frontier models.
  • Rise of Autonomous Agents: Transitioning from passive question-answering to task execution.
  • Enterprise Operationalization: Organizations moving from experimental AI sandboxes to mission-critical operational deployment.

These shifts converge to make the Execution Gap the single largest strategic and economic hurdle for enterprises in the AI era.

Founder Journey & Core Question

Morad’s journey began in information technology and software architecture, paired with deep research into the structural software paradigm shift driven by AI. Studying the gap between raw intelligence and real-world execution led to framing the foundational strategic question behind Ouamarkom:

“How can organizations transition from AI that answers questions and generates text, to AI that executes operations and manages business processes to deliver measurable economic value?”

From this question emerged Ouamarkom's core thesis: The next frontier of artificial intelligence consists of transforming knowledge into structured, measurable economic execution.
